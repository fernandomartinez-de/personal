# Travel & Loyalty Programs

## Trusted Traveler Programs

**TSA PreCheck:** Not enrolled (interested in getting)
**Global Entry:** Not enrolled

**Eligibility on TN Visa:** YES - TN visa holders ARE eligible for both TSA PreCheck and Global Entry
- TSA PreCheck: $78 for 5 years (faster security screening at US airports)
- Global Entry: $100 for 5 years (includes TSA PreCheck + expedited US customs/immigration)
- Application: https://ttp.dhs.gov/ (requires in-person interview)

**Credit card benefits:**
- Chase Sapphire Preferred does NOT include TSA PreCheck/Global Entry credit
- Chase Sapphire Reserve ($550/year) includes $100 Global Entry credit if you upgrade
- Worth paying $100 out-of-pocket for Global Entry given frequent US-Mexico travel

**To Apply:**
1. Create account at ttp.dhs.gov
2. Fill out application (use TN visa info)
3. Pay $78 (PreCheck) or $100 (Global Entry)
4. Schedule interview at airport or enrollment center
5. Bring: Passport, I-94, proof of address
6. Known Traveler Number (KTN) arrives 3-5 days after approval

**Once approved:**
- Add KTN to airline profiles
- Shows on boarding pass as "TSA Pre✓"
- Use TSA PreCheck lanes (keep shoes/belt on, laptop stays in bag)
