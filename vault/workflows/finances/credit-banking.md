## Credit Card Strategy

**Maximize your Chase ecosystem:**
- All points from both Chase cards pool together as Ultimate Rewards
- Use **Sapphire Preferred** for: dining (3x), travel (2-5x), streaming (3x), online groceries (3x)
- Use **Freedom Unlimited** for: everything else (1.5x)
- Redeem points through Sapphire Preferred at 1.25¢ per point for travel OR transfer 1:1 to partners (United, Southwest, Hyatt, etc.)

**Annual strategy checklist:**
- Track Sapphire Preferred $50 hotel credit usage (reset each account anniversary)
- Review annual fee ($95) vs. rewards earned - typically worth it if you spend on dining/travel
- Consider product changing to Sapphire Reserve if you spend $10k+/year on travel/dining (higher fee but 3x travel, $300 travel credit)
