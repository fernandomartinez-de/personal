## Use Restrictions

**Always required for:**
- Employment (I-9, payroll)
- Tax returns
- Bank accounts
- Credit applications
- Government benefits
- Healthcare insurance

**Never share over:**
- Phone (unless YOU initiated the call to known entity)
- Email (unencrypted)
- Text message
- Social media
- Unsecured websites

## Replacement Card

**If lost/stolen:**
1. File police report (if stolen)
2. Contact Social Security Administration: 1-800-772-1213
3. Apply online: ssa.gov/myaccount (if have account)
4. Or visit local SS office with ID (passport)

**Processing time:** 10-14 business days

## Identity Theft Protection

**If SSN compromised:**
1. Place fraud alert: Contact one credit bureau (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion)
2. Review credit reports: annualcreditreport.com
3. Consider credit freeze
4. File identity theft report: identitytheft.gov
5. File IRS Form 14039 (Identity Theft Affidavit)

## SSN for International Use

**Needed for:**
- US tax returns while abroad
- IRS Form 2555 (Foreign Earned Income Exclusion) if working overseas
- FBAR (FinCEN Form 114) if foreign accounts >$10k

**Not needed for:**
- Foreign bank accounts (use passport instead)
- Non-US employment
- Foreign government services
